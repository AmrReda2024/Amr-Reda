A Boutique Cross-Border Practice · Founder-Led · Headquartered in Cairo

Law & Business Synergy is a boutique legal practice headquartered in Cairo and serving clients across the Middle East and North Africa. It was founded on a single conviction: that exceptional legal counsel is not a back-office function — it is a commercial accelerator.

Our role is not merely to identify legal risk, but to engineer pathways that allow business decisions to be executed with confidence, speed, and enforceability. We deliver senior-level legal advisory to international corporations, infrastructure developers, sovereign-backed projects, financial institutions, and growth-stage enterprises operating in — or expanding into — the Arab region’s most complex commercial environments.

Every engagement is led personally by the firm’s Principal, ensuring continuity, accountability, and the strategic depth that mid-market clients rarely access from larger institutional practices. The firm’s signature strength lies at the intersection of FIDIC-based infrastructure contracts, M&A transactions, and the regulatory regimes of Egypt, the UAE, Oman, and Saudi Arabia.

The Principal

The Founder. Vision,

Amr Reda Abdullatif · Founder & Principal · Regional Legal Counsel

Amr Reda Abdullatif is a regional corporate lawyer and infrastructure-contracts specialist with more than eight years of in-house and advisory practice across Egypt, the United Arab Emirates, the Sultanate of Oman, and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

He is the Founder and Principal of Law & Business Synergy, serving concurrently as Regional Legal Counsel to one of the Middle East’s largest construction and infrastructure groups. An Associate of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (ACIArb) and FIDIC-certified dispute-resolution practitioner, Mr. Abdullatif advises governments, multinational contractors, and private investors on cross-border mega-projects — from high-speed railway systems linking the Gulf states to EPC contracts for power, transport, and military infrastructure across MENA.

He is the appointed LCIA YIAG Regional Arbitrator Representative for the Middle East and a contributing author to Lexis® Middle East, IBERIAN LAWYER, and Legalcommunity MENA. His practice combines the precision of common-law contract drafting (TOLES / Cambridge Law Studio) with deep fluency in the civil-law systems of Egypt and the GCC.

Why Law & Business Synergy.
Five reasons clients choose us.
Built Around the Gap Between What Mid-Market Clients Need & What Institutional Firms Deliver

Senior counsel on every matter

Engagements are led personally by the Principal — not delegated to junior associates. Clients receive the strategic judgment of a regional General Counsel without the cost structure of a major institutional firm.

Cross-border by default

Multi-jurisdictional structuring is not an exception in our practice — it is the rule. We routinely harmonise contract terms, governance frameworks, and regulatory filings across the most economically active MENA jurisdictions.

Infrastructure & FIDIC depth

Few practices in the region combine front-end FIDIC drafting expertise with back-end dispute-board and arbitration experience. We protect clients on day one while building the evidentiary record that wins disputes on day one thousand.

Commercial pragmatism

Years of in-house tenure inform every recommendation. Legal advice that ignores commercial timelines, capital constraints, and board dynamics is advice that does not get implemented. We deliver advice that does.

Transparent engagement terms

Clear scopes, predictable fee structures, and direct lines of communication. Our engagements are designed for repeat partnership — not for billable-hour maximisation.
